ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Project Vikas bridges the gap between education and employability by giving students opportunities to develop practical skills and gain experience.

Through internship opportunities across different fields, the initiative helps young people explore their interests, develop professional abilities and gain exposure to real-world work.

Project Vikas also supports career development through webinars, seminars, live shows, resume building and interview preparation.

THE PURPOSE

Opportunity creates possibility.

Students often have knowledge but need practical experience to turn that knowledge into professional confidence.

Project Vikas aims to create those opportunities by connecting learning, skill development and real-world experience.
