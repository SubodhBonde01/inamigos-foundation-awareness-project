ABOUT INAMIGOS

More than an organization. A collective effort for change.

2020
Founded

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO.

01
Section 8 NGO

A registered non-profit organization working for social impact.

∞
Collective Action

Bringing volunteers, communities and partners together for change.

InAmigos Foundation is a non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh and working across India through a network of professionals and volunteers.

Its work focuses on creating meaningful social impact through initiatives in education, community support, animal welfare, women empowerment, environmental sustainability and skill development.

Uniting minds for change.

KEY INITIATIVES

Six initiatives. One purpose.

InAmigos Foundation works across different areas of society, creating opportunities for people, communities, animals and the environment.

01 COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Project Seva

Providing food and clothing support to underprivileged communities while promoting dignity, compassion and care.

Serving humanity ↗
02 EDUCATION

Project Bachpanshala

Bridging educational gaps for underprivileged children through basic digital literacy, life skills and school education support.

Nurturing young minds ↗
03 ANIMAL WELFARE

Project Jeev

Supporting stray and abandoned animals through feeding, protection, rescue and compassionate welfare initiatives.

Every life matters ↗
04 WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development, self-help groups, financial independence and awareness initiatives.

Creating opportunities ↗
05 ENVIRONMENT

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental conservation, sustainability, tree plantation and eco-friendly practices.

Protecting tomorrow ↗
06 SKILL DEVELOPMENT

Project Vikas

Building employability through internships, skill development and career-focused opportunities across multiple fields.

Building futures ↗

Different causes. Different communities. One collective mission.

OUR IMPACT

Change becomes visible when people come together.

InAmigos Foundation works with communities, volunteers and partners to turn meaningful action into measurable social impact.

From education and animal welfare to women empowerment, sustainability and employability — every initiative contributes to a larger movement for change.
