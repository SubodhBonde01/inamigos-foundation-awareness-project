ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Bachpanshala focuses on creating educational opportunities and supporting the learning journey of underprivileged children.

The initiative works towards bridging educational gaps by supporting children with school education, basic digital literacy and essential life skills.

By creating access to learning and encouraging children to develop useful skills, Bachpanshala aims to help young minds move towards a brighter future.

THE PURPOSE

Every child deserves a chance to learn.

Education can open doors to new opportunities. Bachpanshala focuses on helping children who may not have equal access to educational resources.

Along with academic support, the initiative promotes basic digital literacy and life skills that can help children navigate their future with greater confidence.
