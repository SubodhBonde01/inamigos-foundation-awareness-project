PROJECT JEEV Every life matters
ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Project Jeev works towards animal welfare through rescue, protection and feeding initiatives.

The initiative reflects InAmigos Foundation's commitment to protecting animals and responding to their basic needs with compassion and care.

Through the involvement of volunteers and communities, Project Jeev encourages people to recognise that caring for animals is also a responsibility shared by society.

THE PURPOSE

Every life deserves compassion.

Animals can be vulnerable to injury, hunger, abandonment and unsafe living conditions.

Project Jeev focuses on meaningful support through animal rescue, protection and feeding, while encouraging a culture of kindness towards animals.

CARE

Rescue, protection and feeding are at the heart of Project Jeev.

Compassion becomes meaningful when people come together to protect lives that cannot speak for themselves.

BE PART OF THE CHANGE

A little compassion can save a life.
