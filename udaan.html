PROJECT UDAAN Empowering women.

ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Project Udaan works towards empowering women by creating opportunities for skill development and financial independence.

The initiative collaborates with self-help groups in rural areas, helping women develop skills and strengthen their ability to build independent livelihoods.

Udaan also promotes awareness around menstrual hygiene and encourages greater confidence, knowledge and participation within communities.

THE PURPOSE

When women rise, communities grow.

Empowerment begins when people have access to knowledge, practical skills and opportunities to become financially independent.

Project Udaan aims to help women overcome barriers, strengthen their confidence and create better opportunities for themselves and their families.

900+

Women empowered through Project Udaan, according to the foundation's dedicated Udaan initiative article.

Skill development, entrepreneurship, awareness and collective support can create lasting opportunities for women.
